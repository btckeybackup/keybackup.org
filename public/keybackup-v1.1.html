WHAT THIS SHEET IS

This sheet CANNOT move any funds on its own. It is worthless without the seed words on Sheet 2, and the

seed words are worthless without it. Held by two different people, the two halves work like a 2-of-2 lock.

SINGLE-SIG: splitting is the point. Keeping this sheet beside Sheet 2 cancels the passphrase out entirely.

MULTISIG: keeping it beside Sheet 2 is usually correct - one compromised key still cannot spend, and the

far greater risk is that you or your heir never recovers the passphrase at all.

How to choose one

Pick every word with true randomness - dice, or a tool built for the job. A phrase you thought of yourself is not random and will be guessed. Use at least 7 words from the BIP39 English wordlist, or at least 6 words from the EFF long wordlist, or at least 5 words if you are drawing from the whole English dictionary. Run the words together with NO SPACES between them. A space is invisible on paper: an empty box could be a space or could be the end of the passphrase, and nobody reading this later can tell. Letters and digits only, upper or lower case, as long as you record below which you used. Nothing else: no spaces anywhere, no punctuation, no accented letters, no emoji. Those characters are invisible on paper or normalised differently by different wallets.

The passphrase - one character per box, no gaps and no empty boxes

Start in box 1 and fill every box in order until the passphrase ends. Leave no box empty in the middle.

If you write a character wrong, fill that box in solid with black ink and carry on in the next box. A

blacked-out box is not part of the passphrase. Never scribble over a character or write on top of one -

do not leave anything ambiguous. If the sheet ends up with more than the odd blacked-out box, start a

fresh sheet and destroy the old one.

What this is

These sheets back up a Bitcoin wallet. The words on Sheet 2 are the equivalent of the key to a safe. They are not a password that can be reset, and no company anywhere can recover them for you.

Do this first: nothing

There is no deadline and no emergency. Funds do not expire. Rushing is how people lose money here.

Never type, photograph or upload any of these sheets

No legitimate service, app, exchange or support agent will ever ask for the words on Sheet 2 or the passphrase on Sheet 3. Everyone who asks is stealing from you, without exception - including anyone who telephones or emails offering to help with this wallet. Do not scan these sheets, do not photograph them 'just to be safe', do not put them in cloud storage, and do not email them to a relative.

You may be holding only part of it

Sheet 3 is sometimes given to a different person from Sheets 1 and 2. Neither half can move funds alone. Whichever you hold, the contact details on it name the person holding the other.

Find the wallet descriptor backup

If Sheet 1 lists a multisig wallet, the seed words alone are NOT enough. You also need the descriptor backup named on Sheet 1, and the other keys.

Get competent help

Ask a Bitcoin professional you verified independently - a firm with a real address and reputation, never someone who contacted you first. Show them this sheet only. A trustworthy helper never needs to hold your seed words.
