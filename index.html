A paper backup that actually restores your wallet

A printable, self-contained document for recording everything needed to recover a Bitcoin wallet. Not just the seed phrase.

Download the document (v)

Or view it in your browser first. One HTML file. Open it, print it, fill it in by hand. It works offline, makes no network requests, and stores nothing.

Why most Bitcoin backups fail

People back up the seed phrase and stop there. But a seed phrase on its own is often not enough to find your coins. Recovery also needs the wallet configuration: the derivation path, the script type, the passphrase if you set one, and for a multisig wallet, every other co-signer's extended public key.

That configuration usually lives only inside the wallet software. When the laptop dies, the app is discontinued, or someone else is doing the recovering, it is gone, and the seed phrase alone leaves them guessing. Funds are lost this way far more often than they are stolen.

keybackup gives that information a fixed place to live, on paper, in a format a stranger can follow.
